The Studio

Contour Lines is a Paris-based information design studio working at the intersection of data, policy, and storytelling. We help organizations — from the World Bank and UN agencies to climate-tech companies and philanthropic foundations — turn tangled, high-stakes data into interactive narratives that drive real-world decisions.

The Collective

Contour Lines operates as a collective. Depending on what a project demands, Yann draws on a network of specialized associates — data scientists, motion and interactive designers, photogrammetry and computer vision specialists, technical videographers, AI engineers, graphic designers — assembled around the scope of each engagement.

Our Philosophy

Behind every number is a landscape of trade-offs, risks, and human consequence. Design, to us, isn’t decoration for evidence — it’s what gives that evidence enough clarity that it can’t be ignored.
