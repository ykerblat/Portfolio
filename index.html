The Studio

Contour Lines is led by Yann Kerblat, a Paris-based information designer who spent over 15 years as a Disaster & Climate Resilience Analyst for the World Bank and the UN before founding the studio. He holds a Master’s in Data Visualization from Parsons School of Design and works in English and French. The studio works at the intersection of data, policy, and storytelling — helping organizations turn tangled, high-stakes data into interactive narratives that drive real-world decisions.

The Collective

Contour Lines operates as a collective. Depending on what a project demands, Yann draws on a network of specialized associates — data scientists, motion and interactive designers, photogrammetry and computer vision specialists, technical videographers, AI engineers, graphic designers — assembled around the scope of each engagement.

Our Philosophy

Behind every number is a landscape of trade-offs, risks, and human consequence. Design, to us, isn’t decoration for evidence — it’s what gives that evidence enough clarity that it can’t be ignored.
