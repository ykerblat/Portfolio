The Studio

Contour Lines is a Paris-based information design studio working at the intersection of data, policy, and storytelling. We help organizations — from the World Bank and UN agencies to climate-tech companies and philanthropic foundations — turn tangled, high-stakes data into interactive narratives that drive real-world decisions.

The studio is led by Yann Kerblat, who spent over 15 years as a Disaster & Climate Resilience Analyst for the World Bank and the UN before founding Contour Lines, and holds a Master’s from Parsons School of Design. That background shapes how the studio works: less about making data look good, more about making it usable at the moment a decision actually gets made.

The Collective

Contour Lines operates as a collective. Depending on what a project demands, Yann draws on a network of specialized associates — data scientists, motion and interactive designers, photogrammetry and computer vision specialists, technical videographers, AI engineers, graphic designers — assembled around the scope of each engagement.

It’s a structure built for range: interactive dashboards, immersive data narratives, cartographic and spatial analysis, scrollytelling stories, and more.

Our Philosophy

A contour line is how cartographers make the invisible visible — turning elevation into something the eye can read at a glance. We bring that same instinct to data: behind every number is a landscape of trade-offs, risks, and human consequence, and our work is to give that landscape a shape people can see, understand, and act on. Design, to us, isn’t decoration for evidence — it’s what gives evidence enough clarity that it can’t be ignored.

Our Services

Every engagement starts with one question: what does your audience need to walk away knowing? The format follows from there.

Data Stories

Turn a dense report into something people actually read.
We build scrollytelling and immersive narratives that walk an audience through evidence at their own pace — so the issue lands, instead of getting skimmed past.

  • Scrollytelling features
  • Interactive longform reports
  • Narrative-driven microsites

Maps & Spatial Intelligence

See the pattern the spreadsheet was hiding.
From thematic mapping to remote sensing, we turn geographic and spatial data into something a non-specialist can read at a glance — risk exposure, population shifts, environmental change.

  • Interactive web maps
  • GIS-based risk analysis
  • Remote sensing visualizations
  • Cartographic design

Dashboards & Decision Tools

Built for the question a decision-maker is actually facing.
We design dashboards scoped to one clear question — not built to showcase every dataset available.

  • Interactive dashboards
  • KPI tracking tools
  • Country and regional briefing visuals

Outreach & Advocacy

Get your data in front of the people who decide.
For climate tech and other mission-driven ventures, the hardest audience to reach is the one that decides whether you grow — investors, regulators, customers, the public. We translate technical and impact data into outreach built to earn trust.

  • Investor and impact narratives
  • Public-facing explainer content
  • Brand storytelling pieces
  • Advocacy and awareness campaigns
